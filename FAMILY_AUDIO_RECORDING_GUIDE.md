# Shqip Quest v0.3B.3 — Family audio recording guide

This app includes **four previously reviewed Llafazani greeting MP3s**. No new Family MP3s are in the public ZIP yet.

## Generate the next Family recordings

1. Open [Llafazani](https://llafazani.com/en/) and use the voice you previously selected as the male voice.
2. Paste one Albanian expression from the worksheet at a time (not the English or the column heading).
3. Play and verify the **sound, word form, and naturalness** yourself as a fluent speaker. Regenerate anything that sounds incorrect.
4. Save the output as an MP3, and **rename exactly** to the matching `llafazani_<slug>.mp3` listed in the CSV. Plain ASCII filenames avoid Windows/Android issues, while spoken words retain Albanian accents.
5. On your installed preview, go to **Parent → Audio Library → Family pronunciation**. Tick the explicit fluent-speaker check box, then select the MP3s you have checked.
6. Test playback on the learning cards and in any listening questions. The new MP3s are kept in this browser's private IndexedDB, and are **not in GitHub**.

For a quick first batch, record **Nëna, Baba, Motra, Vëllai**. Later, complete all 13.

## Review and release boundaries

- The 13 Family expressions are **not yet approved by you** for this expanded content set. A regional form must not be described as an established Kosovo variant merely because a synthetic voice pronounces it.
- Importing a family MP3 explicitly records that **you reviewed and approved its pronunciation**. The file is stored privately per device, including offline, and is not included in the family profile backup.
- This workflow does **not** upload audio to GitHub or distribute it publicly. Future bundling into a public release requires checking language approval and the recorded source/licensing evidence.
- WevoLabs audio remains private while its redistribution permission is unresolved. Its existing 4 privately imported female clips continue to work.
