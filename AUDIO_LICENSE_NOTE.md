# Llafazani generated-audio rights and provenance record

Reviewed **27 November 2026**.

**Provider:** Llafazani — <https://llafazani.com/en/>

**Official published terms:** <https://llafazani.com/kushtet.html> (marked updated 27 October 2026).

The first provision expressly permits personal and commercial use, including generated audio. The terms also identify the Llafazani voices as AI-generated and identify its Kokoro-82M-based open model. The provider's English homepage advertises saving MP3 or WAV files for videos, podcasts and audiobooks, and use in commercial projects. The underlying Albanian fine-tuned model is labelled Apache-2.0 on its Hugging Face page: <https://huggingface.co/llafazani/kokoro-shqip>.

**Shqip Quest use:** Four distinct generated MP3 files supplied by the project owner, reviewed for pronunciation by a fluent Albanian speaker. They are bundled as static speech clips for an Albanian learning web app and served from a public GitHub Pages repository; a service worker caches those files for offline playback. This interpretation is supported by the provider's current terms, but this note is not legal advice and does not guarantee a permanent, irrevocable licence to all future versions.

**Important disclosure:** These are AI-generated recordings, NOT the recorded voices of specific human speakers. Do not remove any generator-embedded AI provenance metadata. SHA-256 hashes and original names appear in `audio/AUDIO_PROVENANCE.json`.

**Female voice status:** The WevoLabs files are excluded entirely from this release. Free generation does not itself settle public redistribution rights. WevoLabs female audio may remain privately installed in the user's own browser under the previous workflow while its upstream output rights are being clarified.

**Action for release manager:** Retain a dated copy/screenshot of the official source terms and this file. Recheck the terms ahead of a commercial public production release and if there is a material service or licence change.
