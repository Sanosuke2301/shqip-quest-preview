# Shqip Quest — Picture question design guidelines

1. Avoid OS emoji for people whose distinguishing characteristic matters: gender presentation, age, relative size or family context can vary between Android, Windows and iOS.
2. Use a coherent set of original illustrations with distinct facial, clothing, body-proportion and context cues. Test on phone-size screens.
3. **Do not infer family relationships just from appearance.** A woman isn't necessarily a mother, a girl isn't necessarily a sister, and an older adult isn't necessarily a grandparent. For kinship terms, use context/dialogue or text-meaning questions where the distinction can't be shown clearly.
4. Do not display the English answer directly under the correct image. Use neutral visible text such as "Picture A". Preserve descriptive alternative text for accessibility; consider alternate accessible question formats where needed.
5. Check every distractor: wrong options should be visually distinguishable, not unfairly similar in size, age or category.
6. Keep one fixed shuffled order per attempt: no rearranging after the learner has selected an answer or received feedback.
7. Preserve question indices and correct answers when applying purely visual fixes; verify this programmatically.
8. Include all illustration assets in the versioned service worker precache to maintain full offline functionality.
9. Run a complete content leak-and-ambiguity audit (including images, prompts, alt text, labels and scoring) on every new version.
10. Use genuinely original artwork and keep rights/provenance records for any new external illustration assets.
